The Studio

by Rahul Wala

Free tools I use to make my Instagram Reels. They run inside your browser, so your videos never leave your phone or laptop.

Start here

The Studio Sound

Writes captions from your voice, then places sound effects and a music bed that fit what you say, always under your voice. Subtle, Punchy or Off.

Open The Studio Sound How it works Hear the sound library

The Studio Captions

Captions only: 47 templates, stand-out words, and Hindi or Hinglish turned into English captions.

Open The Studio Captions How it works

On an iPhone: open a tool in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app, and your projects are kept.

Both tools are built on tscaps, the open-source caption editor by Franco Zanardi (engine and templates MIT, editor AGPL-3.0). Source code: The Studio Sound and The Studio Captions.

Sound effects are CC0 and the music is public domain, so they're free to use in your Reels.
