Privacy

The Studio by Rahul Wala. Last updated 14 October 2026.

The Studio - Captions and Sound, and The Studio - iPhone

Both tools run entirely in your browser. Your videos, captions and projects stay on your own device: they are not uploaded to any server, and the tools collect no analytics. The speech model and the sound files are downloaded from this website to your browser so they can work offline.

The Studio CLI (Google Drive access)

"The Studio CLI" is a private Google sign-in used only by Rahul Wala, for his own Google account. It asks for access to Google Drive so his own files (finished Reels, tool releases, backups) can be uploaded to, listed in and organised in his own Drive. The sign-in key is stored on Rahul's own laptop.

  • No Google data is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.
  • No Google data is used to train AI models.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The Studio Insights (Instagram)

"The Studio Insights" is a private Meta app used only by Rahul Wala. Through Instagram's official API it reads his own Instagram account's statistics and the public numbers of professional accounts (followers, posts, likes, comments and views), to plan his own Reels. It posts nothing, sends no messages, and is not offered to anyone else. Its key is stored on Rahul's own laptop, and no data is sold, shared or used to train AI models.
